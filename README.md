# Date Tile

A 1x1 home screen widget showing today's date, styled however you like.

- **Layouts**: calendar page, stacked, big date, day & date, circle, month ring, text only, week & day.
- **What shows**: any lines you want, in any order: weekday, date, month, year, week number,
  day of the year, days left, a special day's name, a custom date format, or your own text.
  Each line has its own size, thickness, italic and colour.
- **Look**: font, capitals, shadow, alignment, spacing, shape (rounded, square, circle, squircle,
  none), background colour and opacity, border, header band, progress ring.
- **Changes by day**: accent colour per weekday, per month, or from your wallpaper; a weekend
  colour; special days (birthdays, holidays) with their own colour and name.
- Redraws itself just after midnight.

## Install
1. Open the latest **date-tile-N** release on the Releases page and tap **DateTile.apk**.
2. Open it and allow installing from your browser if asked.
3. Open Date Tile and tap **Add widget**, or long-press the home screen → Widgets → Date Tile.
