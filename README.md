# Clock Out

Tells you when to clock out to reach 8 hours on the clock.

1. Tap **Scan clock rings** and photograph the screen with your rings (TR CODE / TR DATETIME).
2. The app reads the codes and times (on the phone, no internet), shows them so you can check
   or fix them, and works out your clock-out time, counting down live.
3. Time after an **OL** (out to lunch) or **ET** (end) ring isn't counted; you can change which
   codes count as off the clock, and the target (8:00) in Settings.
4. Optional reminder notifications before and at clock-out time.

Install: open the latest **clock-out-N** release on the Releases page and tap **ClockOut.apk**.
