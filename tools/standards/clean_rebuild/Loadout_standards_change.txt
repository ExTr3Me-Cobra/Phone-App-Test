How to read: the % is how much the weight (or reps) needed for the same rank would change.
  Negative = the new standard is lower (easier, your rank goes up). Positive = higher (harder).
  Median = the middle of the scale (Gold 2). Low / high = the 5th and 95th percentile (Wood 2 / Legend).
  Measured at 180 lb body weight for men and 140 lb for women.
  Variations (e.g. hack squat = 0.6 x back squat) keep their multiplier, so they change by the same % as the lift they're based on.
  LOST = no clean source found yet; that exercise would have no rank.
